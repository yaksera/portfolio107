Our Heritage

1989

The Brewery at Mukundapur

Gorkha Brewery is founded in Nawalparasi as a joint venture between the Khetan Group and Carlsberg — the first brewhouse of its scale in the country, and still one of the most advanced in South Asia.

2007

गोर्खा Takes the Country

The Gorkha brand debuts with the soldier's hat on the label and a promise on the neck: dedicated to the brave. Within a few years it is Nepal's number one beer by both volume and value.

2013

Strong Arrives

Gorkha Strong lands at 6% — same brewhouse, same water, more of everything else. Black band, gold ring, 650ml. Made for the end of a long day and the people who earn one.
